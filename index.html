1. Enter Your Bet Numbers
Type six numbers, tap 🎲 LUCKY PICK for random numbers, use the NUMBER BOARD, or scan your ticket below.
📷 Scan or paste your ticket
Tap the box, then choose Scan Text and point the camera at your ticket — or paste the numbers. One bet per line. Check the result before comparing.
2. Enter Winning Numbers
Type the six official winning numbers or select them on the board. You can also paste all six at once (e.g. 05-12-23-34-40-41) into the first box.
📋 Scan or paste official results
On pcso.gov.ph, search the draw date, then select and Copy the results table and paste it here. Or take a screenshot, open it in Photos, press and hold the text to select it, Copy, and paste here. Results are saved on this device and fill the winning numbers when you pick a game and draw date.
NEW DRAW clears only the selected game. CLEAR ALL GAMES clears all five (saved official results are kept).
